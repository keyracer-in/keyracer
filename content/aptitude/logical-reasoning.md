# 🧠 Logical Reasoning

## 🎯 Overview

Logical Reasoning is a fundamental skill that evaluates your ability to think systematically, analyze information objectively, and draw valid conclusions from given premises.

### 🌟 Why is Logical Reasoning Essential?

**🎓 Academic Excellence:**
- Core component of entrance exams (CAT, GMAT, LSAT, GRE)
- Essential for competitive government exams
- Critical for research and analytical studies

**💼 Professional Success:**
- Decision-making in business and management
- Problem-solving in engineering and technology
- Critical analysis in law and consulting

## 🔢 Number Series

**Types of Series:**
- **Arithmetic**: Constant difference between terms
- **Geometric**: Constant ratio between terms
- **Square/Cube**: Based on powers of numbers
- **Mixed**: Combination of patterns

> **Example**: Find next number: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?
> 
> **Solution**:
> - Differences: 3, 5, 7, 9 (odd numbers)
> - Next difference: 11
> - 26 + 11 = 37
> 
> **Answer**: 37

---

## 🔤 Letter Series

**Pattern Types:**
- **Alphabetical Order**: Forward/backward movement
- **Position-based**: Using letter positions (A=1, B=2...)
- **Skip Patterns**: Letters skipping positions
- **Vowel-Consonant**: Alternating patterns

> **Example**: Find next letter: A, C, E, G, ?
> 
> **Solution**:
> - Pattern: Every second letter in alphabet
> - A, C, E, G, I
> 
> **Answer**: I

## 🔷 Figure Series

**Pattern Types:**
- **Rotation**: Figures rotating clockwise/counterclockwise
- **Reflection**: Mirror images or flips
- **Addition/Subtraction**: Elements being added or removed
- **Size Changes**: Figures growing or shrinking
- **Position Changes**: Elements moving within the figure

**Key Strategies:**
- Observe each element separately
- Look for patterns in shape, size, position, and orientation
- Check for symmetry and rotation
- Count elements (lines, dots, shapes)

> **Example**: In a series of squares, each has one more dot than the previous. If the 3rd square has 5 dots, how many in the 5th?
> 
> **Solution**:
> - Pattern: Adding 2 dots each time
> - 3rd square: 5 dots
> - 4th square: 7 dots
> - 5th square: 9 dots
> 
> **Answer**: 9 dots

> 💡 **Tip**: Break complex figures into simpler components - analyze each part separately!

## 🔐 Coding-Decoding

> **Common Patterns**:
> - Simple substitution (A→B, B→C)
> - Position-based (A=1, B=2, C=3...)
> - Reverse alphabet (A=Z, B=Y, C=X...)
> - Mathematical operations (+1, -1, +2, -2...)

**Key Strategies:**
- Find the pattern in given examples
- Apply same pattern to new words
- Check for consistency across all examples

> **Example**: If CAT = FDW, then DOG = ?
> 
> **Solution**:
> - C+3=F, A+3=D, T+3=W
> - Pattern: Each letter +3
> - D+3=G, O+3=R, G+3=J
> 
> **Answer**: GRJ

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Blood Relations

**Family Relationships:**
- **Direct**: Father, mother, son, daughter, brother, sister
- **Indirect**: Uncle, aunt, nephew, niece, grandfather, grandmother
- **In-laws**: Father-in-law, mother-in-law, brother-in-law, sister-in-law

**Solving Strategy:**
1. Draw family tree diagrams
2. Identify generation levels
3. Trace relationships step by step

> **Example**: Pointing to a man, woman says "His mother is the only daughter of my father." Relation?
> 
> **Solution**:
> - Woman's father has only daughter = woman herself
> - Man's mother = woman
> - So man is woman's son
> 
> **Answer**: Son

## 🧭 Direction Sense

> **Basic Directions**: North(N), South(S), East(E), West(W)
> 
> **Intermediate**: Northeast(NE), Northwest(NW), Southeast(SE), Southwest(SW)
> 
> **Opposite Directions**: N↔S, E↔W

> **Example**: A man walks 5 km north, then 3 km east. How far from starting point?
> 
> **Solution**:
> - Use Pythagoras: √(5² + 3²) = √(25+9) = √34 ≈ 5.83 km
> 
> **Answer**: √34 km or approximately 5.83 km

## 📊 Syllogisms

**Logical Statements:**
- **Universal Affirmative (A)**: All S are P
- **Universal Negative (E)**: No S are P
- **Particular Affirmative (I)**: Some S are P
- **Particular Negative (O)**: Some S are not P

> **Example**: All roses are flowers. Some flowers are red. Conclusion?
> 
> **Solution**: Some roses may be red (possible)
> 
> **Answer**: Some roses may be red
