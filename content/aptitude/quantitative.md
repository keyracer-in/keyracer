## 📈 Percentages

> **Formula**: Percentage = (Part/Whole) × 100

**Key Concepts:**
- Percentage means "per hundred" (%)
- To convert fraction to %: multiply by 100
- To convert % to fraction: divide by 100

> **Example 1**: What is 25% of 400?
> 
> **Solution**: (25/100) × 400 = 100
> 
> **Answer**: 100

> **Example 2**: Express 3/5 as percentage
> 
> **Solution**: (3/5) × 100 = 60%
> 
> **Answer**: 60%

> 💡 **Tip**: For quick calculations, remember that 25% = 1/4, 50% = 1/2, 75% = 3/4

## 💰 Profit & Loss

> **Key Formulas**:
> - Profit = SP - CP
> - Loss = CP - SP
> - Profit % = (Profit/CP) × 100
> - Loss % = (Loss/CP) × 100

**Important Terms:**
- **Cost Price (CP)**: Original price of item
- **Selling Price (SP)**: Price at which item is sold
- **Profit**: When SP > CP
- **Loss**: When SP < CP

> **Example**: Shopkeeper buys shirt for ₹800, sells for ₹1000. Find profit %.
> 
> **Solution Steps:**
> 1. Calculate Profit: ₹1000 - ₹800 = ₹200
> 2. Apply Formula: (200/800) × 100 = 25%
> 
> **Answer**: 25%

## 🏦 Interest Calculations

### Simple Interest

> **Formula**: SI = (P × R × T) / 100
> 
> **Amount**: A = P + SI
> 
> Where:
> - P = Principal (initial amount)
> - R = Rate (interest rate per annum)
> - T = Time (duration in years)
> - A = Amount (principal + interest)

> **Example**: Find SI on ₹5000 at 8% per annum for 3 years.
> 
> **Solution**:
> SI = (5000 × 8 × 3) / 100 = ₹1200
> 
> **Answer**: ₹1200

### Compound Interest

> **Formula**: CI = P(1 + R/100)^T - P
> 
> **Amount**: A = P(1 + R/100)^T

> **Example**: Find CI on ₹10000 at 10% per annum for 2 years.
> 
> **Solution**:
> 1. Calculate Amount: A = 10000(1 + 10/100)^2 = 10000 × 1.1 × 1.1 = ₹12100
> 2. Calculate CI: CI = ₹12100 - ₹10000 = ₹2100
> 
> **Answer**: ₹2100

> ⚠️ **Warning**: For same P, R, T, CI is always greater than SI. The difference increases with time.

## ⚙️ Time & Work

> **Key Formula**: If A does work in x days, A's 1 day work = 1/x
> 
> **Combined Work**: When working together, individual rates are added

**Important Concepts:**
- Work rate = Work done per unit time
- When working together, individual rates are added
- Time = Total Work / Combined Rate

> **Example**: A can do work in 10 days, B in 15 days. How long together?
> 
> **Solution Steps:**
> 1. A's 1 day work = 1/10
> 2. B's 1 day work = 1/15
> 3. Together = 1/10 + 1/15 = (3+2)/30 = 5/30 = 1/6
> 4. Time = 6 days
> 
> **Answer**: 6 days

## 🚗 Time, Speed & Distance

> **Basic Formula**: Speed = Distance / Time
> 
> **Relative Speed**:
> - Same direction: |v1 - v2|
> - Opposite direction: v1 + v2
> 
> **Average Speed**: Total Distance / Total Time

> ✓ **Important**: Average speed ≠ Average of speeds

> **Example**: Car travels 150 km at 50 km/h, then 100 km at 40 km/h. Find average speed.
> 
> **Solution**:
> 1. Time1 = 150/50 = 3h
> 2. Time2 = 100/40 = 2.5h
> 3. Total time = 5.5h, Total distance = 250 km
> 4. Average speed = 250/5.5 = 45.45 km/h
> 
> **Answer**: 45.45 km/h

## 🔢 Number Systems

> **Prime Numbers**: Numbers with exactly two factors (1 and itself)
> 
> **HCF × LCM**: Product of two numbers

**Important Concepts:**
- **Prime**: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23...
- **Composite**: Numbers with more than 2 factors
- **HCF**: Highest Common Factor
- **LCM**: Least Common Multiple

> **Example**: Find HCF of 24 and 36
> 
> **Solution**:
> - Factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24
> - Factors of 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36
> - Common factors: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12
> - HCF = 12
> 
> **Answer**: 12

### Divisibility Rules

- **Div by 2**: Last digit even (0,2,4,6,8)
- **Div by 3**: Sum of digits divisible by 3
- **Div by 4**: Last two digits divisible by 4
- **Div by 5**: Last digit 0 or 5
- **Div by 6**: Divisible by both 2 and 3
- **Div by 9**: Sum of digits divisible by 9
- **Div by 10**: Last digit 0

## 📐 Algebra

### Linear Equations

> **One Variable**: ax + b = 0 ⇒ x = -b/a
> 
> **Two Variables**: Use substitution or elimination method

> **Example**: Solve 3x + 7 = 22
> 
> **Solution**:
> 1. 3x = 22 - 7 = 15
> 2. x = 15/3 = 5
> 
> **Answer**: x = 5

### Quadratic Equations

> **Standard Form**: ax² + bx + c = 0
> 
> **Roots**: x = [-b ± √(b²-4ac)] / 2a
> 
> **Sum of roots**: -b/a
> 
> **Product of roots**: c/a

> **Example**: Solve x² - 5x + 6 = 0
> 
> **Solution**:
> - Factor: (x-2)(x-3) = 0
> - x = 2 or x = 3
> 
> **Answer**: x = 2, 3

## 📝 Quick Tips & Shortcuts

1. **Percentage shortcuts**: 10% = 1/10, 25% = 1/4, 50% = 1/2, 75% = 3/4
2. **Profit/Loss**: When SP = CP, no profit/loss
3. **SI vs CI**: For same P,R,T, CI > SI
4. **Work problems**: If A does in x days, B in y days, together = xy/(x+y) days
5. **Average speed**: Use harmonic mean for equal distance different speeds
6. **Prime checking**: Check divisibility up to √n
7. **HCF**: Use Euclidean algorithm
8. **LCM formula**: LCM = Product/HCF

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Practice mental math daily to improve speed and accuracy!
